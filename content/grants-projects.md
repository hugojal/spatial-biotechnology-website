# Grants & Funded Projects

The Spatial Biotechnology Laboratory is supported by prestigious national and international competitive funding agencies and foundations.

---

## 1. ERC Proof of Concept Grant – EVOaware
- **Funding Body:** European Research Council (ERC)
- **PI:** Xavier Rovira-Clavé, PhD
- **Project Focus:** Development of the **EVOaware** platform, designed to address and anticipate tumour resistance to targeted therapies and accelerate the discovery and preclinical development of next-generation cancer treatments.

---

## 2. Spanish Association Against Cancer (AECC) – 2025 Grant Programme
- **Funding Body:** Asociación Española Contra el Cáncer (AECC)
- **PI:** Xavier Rovira-Clavé, PhD
- **Project Focus:** Advanced spatial cell therapy platforms and high-content interrogation of clonal cooperative architectures in paediatric and adult solid tumours.

---

## 3. Human Frontier Science Program (HFSP) – SOLFEGE Consortium
- **Funding Body:** Human Frontier Science Program (HFSP)
- **Consortium Partners:** German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ, Heidelberg - Lead), Institute for Bioengineering of Catalonia (IBEC - Xavier Rovira-Clavé), Duke University (John Hickey).
- **Project Focus:** **SOLFEGE** explores how distinct cell types coordinate via soluble signaling factors in the dynamic tumor microenvironment across multiple spatial and temporal dimensions.

---

## 4. IBEC Strategic Emerging Groups Programme
- **Funding Body:** Institute for Bioengineering of Catalonia (IBEC) / Generalitat de Catalunya / CERCA
- **Project Focus:** Establishment of state-of-the-art Multiplexed Ion Beam Imaging (MIBI) infrastructure and high-throughput spatial perturbation pipelines at the Barcelona Science Park (PCB).
